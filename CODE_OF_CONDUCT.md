# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want everybody to take part in this project without harassment. That applies
whatever your experience, gender, identity, disability, appearance, race,
religion, or background.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the project:

- Use welcoming language.
- Respect a different point of view.
- Accept constructive review.
- Put the community first.

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, and political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing somebody's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@bunny.net**. The maintainers review
every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and respond.

A maintainer who does not follow this code faces the same consequences as
anybody else.
